Cookies &
analytics.

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Current default

This website does not set analytics cookies by default. It uses no advertising trackers, and no analytics script is loaded while the analytics measurement ID remains blank in the site code.

Optional analytics

If Google Analytics is later configured, a consent notice will appear before the analytics script is loaded. Choosing “Allow analytics” stores your choice in this browser's local storage and enables analytics. Choosing “No thanks” stores the rejection choice and leaves analytics disabled.

Change your choice

You can clear this site's local storage in your browser settings to reset a saved analytics choice. If analytics is not configured, the consent notice will not appear.

Third-party links

External websites may use their own cookies and tracking technologies after you leave this site. Check their policies directly.
